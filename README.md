## 👨‍💻 About Me

> *"From understanding the data to building AI applications I can explain."*

Hey there! 👋 I'm **Nguyễn Đức Trí**, an undergraduate **Artificial Intelligence student** at **HUFLIT** (Ho Chi Minh City University of Foreign Languages & Information Technology), interested in **Machine Learning**, **NLP**, and **applied AI systems**.

- 🎓 **Education:** Undergraduate in **Artificial Intelligence** @ **HUFLIT** *(In Progress)*
- 🎯 **Aspiration:** Becoming an **AI Engineer** who connects models with useful applications
- 🔭 **What I'm Learning & Building:**
  - **Machine Learning:** data preprocessing, EDA, classification, and Logistic Regression from scratch
  - **Model Evaluation:** cross-validation, ROC / PR curves, and validation-based threshold selection
  - **Deep Learning & NLP:** studying CNNs, word embeddings, and multi-label emotion classification
  - **LLM / RAG:** exploring grounded Q&A, embeddings, vector search, and tool-calling architectures
  - **AI Applications:** integrating model providers into backend APIs and full-stack workflows
- ⚡ **Mindset:**
  - 🧠 Understand the idea, implement it, then inspect where it fails
  - 🔍 Keep training, validation, and test decisions separate
  - 🛠️ Build with reproducibility, clear APIs, and access control in mind
  - 🌱 Open to **AI Engineer Internship** opportunities
